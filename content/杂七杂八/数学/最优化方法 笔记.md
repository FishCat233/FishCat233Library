泰勒定理

泰勒展开

Suppose that \(f(x)\) is differentiable at \(\bar{x}\). If there is a vector \(d\) such that \(\nabla f(\bar{x})^T d < 0\), then for all \(\lambda > 0\) and sufficiently small, \(f(\bar{x} + \lambda d) < f(\bar{x})\), and hence \(d\) is a descent direction of \(f(x)\) at \(\bar{x}\).
